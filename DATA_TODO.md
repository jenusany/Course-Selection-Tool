## Intentionally synthetic (not real Western data)

- **All `Section` rows (timetable data)** — days/times, room, instructor,
  capacity, enrolled count. There is no real Western timetable feed available
  to mock against (that lives entirely inside Student Center/PeopleSoft), so
  every section is procedurally generated by
  `packages/db/prisma/seed/lib/sections.ts` and flagged `synthetic: true` on
  the `Section` model. Good enough to exercise the schedule builder UI and
  conflict detection; not representative of Western's actual Fall/Winter
  timetable.
- **Seeded student histories** (`packages/db/prisma/seed/students.ts`) —
  names, emails, and course/grade histories for the 8 fixture students are
  fabricated to hit specific test scenarios (on-track, near-graduation,
  antirequisite conflict, active hold, double-module, mid-program, borderline
  honours average). Real course codes and grades are used to keep them
  internally consistent with the requirement rules, but these are not real
  students or transcripts.

## Scope simplifications

- **Breadth Category A electives** (`PSYCHOL 1000`, `ECONOMIC 1021A/B`,
  `ECONOMIC 1022A/B`) are outside the Faculty of Science and were seeded
  minimally — title, credit weight, breadth category only, no full
  prerequisite/antirequisite structure — purely so the Category A breadth
  requirement is testable end-to-end. Category B is covered by the (fully
  seeded) `WRITING` courses. Since every Science subject sits in Category C
  per the calendar's own Breadth Requirements page, a Science-only catalog
  can never demonstrate Category A/B without a token elective or two from
  outside Science; this is that token set, not a claim of Faculty of Arts &
  Humanities / Social Science course-catalog coverage.
- **"Basic Medical Sciences disciplines" course pool** — the Honours
  Specialization in Biology module allows courses from Anatomy and Cell
  Biology, Biochemistry, Epidemiology and Biostatistics, Medical
  Bioinformatics, Medical Biophysics, Medical Sciences, Microbiology and
  Immunology, One Health, Pathology, Physiology, Physiology and Pharmacology,
  and Pharmacology to count toward the "4.0 courses at the 2200 level or
  above" requirement. None of these disciplines' courses are seeded (out of
  the ~80-120 course budget and out of Faculty-of-Science-only scope); the
  requirement's "courses that would satisfy this" suggestions will therefore
  only ever surface Biology-department options, which is a known v1
  limitation, not a bug.
- **Essay designation and breadth category are derived from documented
  calendar rules, not scraped per-course.** This is exact (the Course
  Numbering Policy page states essay status is fully determined by the
  course-number suffix letter, and the Breadth Requirements page lists every
  subject under exactly one category), not a heuristic — noted here only so
  it's clear these two fields weren't independently fetched per course.
- **Some minor per-module rules are advisory notes, not enforced logic** in
  v1 (e.g. the Software Engineering/Game Development Minor substitution rule
  in the HSp Computer Science module, the "4999E can substitute" rule in the
  HSp Biology module, the "68% + Dean's permission" exceptional-circumstances
  clause). Each is called out in its module's YAML under `notes:`. The audit
  engine (Phase 2) should surface these as advisory text when the triggering
  condition is detected, rather than silently ignoring them.

## Curated course selection

The full course lists for COMPSCI (72), BIOLOGY (92), MATH (65), and several
other subjects were scraped in full, then curated down to ~120 courses total
(named in the four seeded modules' requirements, plus a realistic elective
spread for browsing/search). The uncurated full lists are not committed to
the repo; if broader catalog coverage is wanted later, re-scraping is
straightforward (subject listing pages are plain HTML at
`westerncalendar.uwo.ca/Courses.cfm?Subject=<CODE>&SelectedCalendar=Live&ArchiveID=`).
